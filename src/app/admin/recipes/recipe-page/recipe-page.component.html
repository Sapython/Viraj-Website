Preparation

  • Add 2spoon of oil.
  • Heat oil in a pan and fry bread pieces till they turn crispy.
  • Add boiled potatoes to it.
  • Cut the potatoes into small cubes.
  • Add chana, dahi, green chutney and imli chutney.
  • Add salt, chaat masala, black pepper powder and red
  • chilli powder.Finally, garnish with some sev and serve.
  • Have the chaat before the fried breads get soggy.
